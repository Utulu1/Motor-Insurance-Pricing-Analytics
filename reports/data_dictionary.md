# Data Dictionary

## Purpose

This document describes the variables used throughout the Motor Insurance Pricing Analytics project. The data dictionary provides a common business and technical understanding of each feature used in the actuarial pricing analysis.

---

# Dataset

Dataset: French Motor Third-Party Liability (freMTPL2)

Purpose: Motor insurance claim frequency modelling

Target Variable: Number of Claims

---

# Variable Definitions

| Variable | Description | Data Type | Business Importance |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|---------------------|
| IDpol | Unique policy identifier | Integer | Identifies each insurance policy |
| ClaimNb | Number of claims reported during the policy period | Integer | Target variable for claim frequency modelling |
| Exposure | Length of policy exposure measured in years | Continuous | Used as the offset variable within the Poisson GLM |
| Area | Geographic region where the policyholder resides | Categorical | Captures geographical variation in insurance risk |
| VehPower | Vehicle engine power category | Integer | Represents vehicle performance and potential risk |
| VehAge | Vehicle age in years | Integer | Older vehicles may exhibit different claim behaviour |
| DrivAge | Driver age in years | Integer | A key actuarial rating factor influencing claim frequency |
| BonusMalus | Driver's Bonus-Malus coefficient | Continuous | Represents previous driving experience and claims history |
| VehBrand | Vehicle manufacturer or brand | Categorical | Captures differences in vehicle characteristics |
| VehGas | Fuel type of the vehicle | Categorical | Used to distinguish vehicle operating characteristics |
| Density | Population density of the policyholder's location | Continuous | Proxy for traffic exposure and accident likelihood |
| Region | Administrative region | Categorical | Captures regional differences in claim frequency |

---

# Target Variable

**ClaimNb**

The objective of the model is to estimate the expected number of insurance claims generated by each policy while accounting for differences in exposure.

---

# Offset Variable

**Exposure**

Exposure represents the proportion of a full policy year during which the insured vehicle was covered.

Within the Poisson Generalized Linear Model, the natural logarithm of exposure was incorporated as an offset to model claim frequency rather than raw claim counts.

---

# Data Types

The project contains a mixture of:

- Numerical variables
- Continuous variables
- Categorical variables
- Count variables

Categorical variables were appropriately encoded before modelling.

---

# Business Relevance

Each variable contributes to understanding the relationship between policyholder characteristics and expected claim frequency. Together they provide the information required to estimate insurance risk and support actuarial pricing decisions.

---

# Conclusion

The variables included within the modelling dataset provide comprehensive information describing policyholder characteristics, vehicle attributes, geographical exposure and historical risk indicators. These variables form the foundation of the Poisson GLM developed in this project.
